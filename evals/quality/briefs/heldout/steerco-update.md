# Brief: finance systems programme, month nine steering committee

The finance transformation lead needs the month-nine update for the programme
steering committee of a fictional manufacturer, Orrin Components. The programme
replaces three regional ledgers with one finance system over eighteen months.

- **Audience.** The steering committee: the CFO (chair), two regional finance
  directors, the CIO and the programme sponsor. Forty-five minutes, deck sent
  the day before.
- **Decision.** Whether to approve moving the second go-live wave from month 14
  to month 16 and releasing $1.2m of contingency to fund it.
- **Constraints.** Status must be shown against the baseline the committee
  approved in month one, not against the last re-plan. Risks need an owner and
  a date. Do not soften the two red workstreams.
- **Length.** 10 to 12 pages.

Status at month nine (budget $14.0m, spent $8.9m, forecast at completion $15.1m
without the change, $15.3m with it; contingency remaining $2.1m):

| Workstream | Baseline finish | Forecast finish | Status | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Design and build | Month 8 | Month 9 | Amber | Two integrations late |
| Data migration | Month 11 | Month 14 | Red | Third mock load failed reconciliation by 2.4% |
| Testing | Month 12 | Month 14 | Red | Blocked by migration; 61% of scripts run |
| Training | Month 13 | Month 13 | Green | 420 of 900 users trained |
| Wave 1 go-live (Americas) | Month 10 | Month 10 | Green | Cutover rehearsal passed |
| Wave 2 go-live (Europe, Asia) | Month 14 | Month 16 if approved | Amber | Depends on migration |

Top risks: the European works council has not yet been consulted on the new
approval workflow (owner: Europe finance director); the legacy Asia ledger's
support contract ends in month 15 (owner: CIO).

Why this brief is in the suite: it is held out. A status deck is where a
decision gets buried under a traffic-light table. A good run leads with the
decision and its cost, shows slippage against the approved baseline, and makes
the contract end date the reason month 16 is not free.
