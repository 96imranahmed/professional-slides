# Executive Synthesis

The executive synthesis is a distinct slide type. It is not an agenda, contents page, tracker, scorecard, RAG dashboard, or collection of status cards.

## Use when

Use it near the start of an executive pre-read when the audience needs the answer before the supporting analysis. Give it the visible structural label **Executive summary**.

Do not force it into an existing deck unless the requested revision authorizes the structural change.

## Content

The page needs:

1. one governing answer in the title;
2. two to four supporting branches;
3. proof for each branch;
4. the decision consequence of each branch; and
5. one overall action, condition, or recommendation.

Each branch must be a complete thought. A verdict without proof is not synthesis.

## Layout

Use an answer spine by default:

- left: branch conclusion;
- centre: proof;
- right: consequence;
- bottom: one overall decision.

Use one accent colour plus neutrals. Do not colour branches red, amber, and green. Do not add a tracker or repeated status strip.

Alternative layouts are acceptable only when they preserve the same logic, such as parallel peer branches or situation, complication, resolution.

## Check

- The title states the overall answer.
- Every branch includes conclusion, proof, and consequence.
- The bottom line gives one explicit action or condition.
- The page cannot be mistaken for navigation or status reporting.
- The close of the deck still aligns with this answer.
