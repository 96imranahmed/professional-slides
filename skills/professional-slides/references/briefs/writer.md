# Writer brief: a section's pages in full

You write each page of one section complete - its spine detail and its copy - against the limits printed beside it. The critique has settled the argument: keep every title, type, `settles`, `evidence` and the measures a page shows as they are, or the critique must read the page again (`SPINE_LOCKED`). Sources: [Writing a page](../page-types.md#writing-a-page), [The evidence contract](../page-types.md#the-evidence-contract), [Body copy](../copy.md#body-copy), [Where each kind of sentence goes](../copy.md#where-each-kind-of-sentence-goes), [Punctuation and voice](../copy.md#punctuation-and-voice), [Copy QA](../copy.md#copy-qa).

- **Every analytical page carries** `type`, `form`, `commentary` (where the explanation lives), `why`, `settles` or `evidence`, `adds` (what the commentary says that the exhibit cannot; required with `beside`, `beside-left`, `below`, `captions`, `on-exhibit`), the content keys, and `source`.
- **Numbers by reference, never typed.** Every number of the insight log is printed by a token or a measure: `{{<insight>/<measure>@<period or member> | <format>}}` in any text, `series: [{ measure }]` with `select` on a chart, a metric's `measure` and `format`. The tokens below are exact and their formats state the value; a scale word after a token ("million") must match the measure's unit. A number computed from measures (a growth, a gap, a share) is an analysis with its own token, not arithmetic in the copy.
- **Hold the limits** printed for the page's type, form and commentary: words per block, lines per title, takeaway and source, exhibits per page. A floor is as binding as a ceiling.
- **Copy:** each point adds what the exhibit cannot show - a cause, a consequence, a comparison - in plain declarative sentences; no restating the title, no planning voice ("we will show"), no hedges. Lead each point with its finding; one idea per point.
- **Check, then hand back** only when the page check reports nothing blocking on your pages; answer the questions it stages (judge) or hand them back with the page ids.
