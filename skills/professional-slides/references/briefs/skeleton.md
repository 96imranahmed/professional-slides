# Skeleton brief: a section's titles, types, evidence and settles

You write one section of the spine: for each page its `title`, `type`, `evidence` and `settles` (and `why`), nothing drawn. The critique reads only these, so copy and exhibits written now are thrown away when the spine moves. Sources: [The one-page core](../storylining.md#the-one-page-core), [Action titles](../copy.md#action-titles), [Writing a page](../page-types.md#writing-a-page), [The pages file is the dot-dash](../page-types.md#the-pages-file-is-the-dot-dash).

- **The section proves its pillar.** Its divider's title states the pillar's claim; every page title is a child of it, answering "why?" or "how do we know?" of it. A page that proves another pillar's claim belongs in that section.
- **A title states the answer, not the topic:** subject, verb of change or state, magnitude or comparator; 15 words at most (`TITLE_WORDS`), about 10 as the norm, two lines at most. Readable numerals, no hedges (*may, some, potentially, a range of*). Where the evidence is thinner than the wording, narrow the claim and keep its direction; never title a page with what the evidence lacks.
- **A title's claim holds against the record, not the page.** A ranked or universal word (best, leads, only, every) holds against the whole set the insight records, not the members the page shows; two figures set against each other share one definition, population, period and edition; and no figure appears that the record lacks, or at a precision it lacks. The items and hazards printed under the deck (from the answer critique) bind every title: no page makes those comparisons as stated.
- **One page per strong insight.** `evidence` names the insight ids the page rests on (listed below). A page with no strong insight under it is a candidate for the appendix, not padding for the body.
- **`settles` names what the page shows:** `{ kind, what, measures: ["<insight>/<measure>", ...] }`, each measure one listed under its insight below, and only those the page draws (`kind`: `count`, `share`, `rank`, `rate`, `sequence`, `comparison`, `structure`). The critique is bound to the measures a page shows; a page whose numbers name no measure is refused as `SPINE_UNDETERMINED`. Only a page whose evidence is statements takes `{ kind: "qualitative", what }`, and a scorecard or a chart never does.
- **Say how each measure is shown, where the spine can't leave it to the layout.** A measure with a hole (a period or member with no number) is drawn only over the ones it has: declare that view with an exhibit stub, `series: [{ measure, name }]` with `select: { periods | members }`. A chart plots 8 values or more; fewer, and the page states them as a `numbers` page. Two exhibits in one unit over the same members say what is read between them: `settles.relation: { kind: gap | ratio | level | separate, reason }`. `--scaffold <type> --evidence <insight> --id <page>` prints the stub bound to the measures. When it says `Not bound`, its numbers are a worked example's: follow its reason, and never copy them.
- **`type` is the reading task** the claim asks of the reader (`--types`); `why` says in one sentence why it fits this claim. Leave `form` to the plan unless the claim needs one.
- **Do not write** exhibits, points, takeaways or copy: the writer does that once the critique is ready.
- Stay inside your section's part file. Page ids are stable: keep them, and add new pages with fresh ids.
