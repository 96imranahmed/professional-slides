# Brief: London or New York

Someone with an offer in both cities wants to know where to live and work.

- **Audience.** One person, deciding in the next month.
- **Decision.** Which city, and which area inside it.
- **Ranked criteria.** Total compensation after tax and housing; commute;
  schools; the cost of leaving again.
- **Constraints.** Every comparison on one basis, in one currency, for one
  household size. Say which figures are published and which are modelled.
- **Length.** About 30 pages.

Why this brief is in the suite: it is geography, and a deck about places whose
pages are paragraphs beside a stock photograph of a skyline is the failure the
annotated map exists to fix. A good run puts boroughs on a map with real
boundaries and the sentence about each one keyed to it.
