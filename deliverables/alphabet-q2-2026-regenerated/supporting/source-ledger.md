## Evidence classifications and gaps

- **Reported:** company tables and explicitly attributed management disclosures from S1/S2. **Derived:** arithmetic on reported data. **Analyst normalization:** gain-stripped EPS and SBC economic-cash proxy, each labeled. **Illustrative scenario:** all growth, margin, capital-spend, discount-rate, recovery and loss inputs in the DCF.
- There is no independent query-market-share, AI revenue-per-query, AI unit-cost, organic Cloud, TPU sales breakdown or Cloud cohort-renewal series in the admitted evidence. Current growth cannot settle these durability tests. Their absence lowers confidence in extrapolation and becomes a review condition.
- No numeric full-year capex guidance is used. The 10-Q states that infrastructure investment will increase significantly; all model capex values are analyst scenario assumptions. Search snippets of later or additional call material were not substituted for the specified documents.
- A September share count and July/August cash roll-forward are not supplied by the cutoff documents. The model deliberately freezes June balances and shares, and labels the resulting September valuation as a frozen-information sensitivity rather than a current balance-sheet estimate.
- All material assumption weaknesses, including DCF terminal dependence, financing treatment, commitment overlap and asset haircuts, are carried into `assumptions-and-model.md` and the proposed audience slides.
