# Components

Every reusable slide element is a registered component in `runtime/registry.mjs`. Components own their internal geometry and consume theme tokens; the composition tree owns where they sit. Give each one a clear audience job, and use one shared definition wherever it repeats.

## Page furniture

**`page-template`** renders furniture only: footer rule options `none`, `bottom`, `top-and-bottom`, plus a footer company name or a top-right logo slot. `contentSpacing: "compact"` reserves `space.5` below the measured title for a dense evidence family. **`slide-chrome`** composes that furniture with the shared action title (`titleVariant`, `with-line` or `without-line`). **`source`** sits bottom left on the page-number baseline and names publisher, document or dataset, and the relevant date; full URLs, locators and calculation notes live in speaker notes. **`footnote`** carries the one methodology note a page needs, at source size, above the source line. **`page-number`** sits bottom right, with the company name immediately to its left when used. Wrapped sources extend upward and the body frame contracts to match.

**`action-title`** and **`section-title`** take `text` and `variant` (`with-line`, `without-line`, default without). Both hold the same anchor, type and allocated space; the rule sits `space-2` below the measured last line. **`chart-title`** takes `heading`, optional `unit`, optional `badge` and `variant` (`underlined` default, or `unit`), with `headerBandHeight` to align wrapped peers in a group. The heading sits on the first line in the heading role and the unit on a second, compact grey line inside the same band (`Percent, n = 240`, `$bn`, `Index, 2021 = 100`); a `badge` (the CAGR pill the composer builds from `cagr`) sits at the band's right. The heading names measure, population and period and carries no statistics, so `NYPD reported homicides` rather than `NYPD: 382 to 305`; the unit line may carry a base year or sample size.

**`section`** (`open`, `muted`, `primary`) owns a surface, padding and a nested composition; **`section-heading`** (`standard`, `accent`, `inverse`) is its heading leaf, text only. **`section-boundary`** takes `variant` alone: `inference` (quiet divider interrupted by a right-pointing disc chevron, for a supported inference), `inference-chevron` (dashed divider stopping above and below a centred shaftless chevron, sized to the bullet content), `related` (dashed divider for adjacent context) and `subsection` (horizontal separator between cohesive groups inside a panel).

## Covers and navigation

**`cover`** takes a short `title`, optional `subtitle`, `date` and `logo`, freezes measured line breaks, and rejects copy beyond two lines per field. `dark` (default) fills the page in the deck's navy with the logo top-left, an accent rule and the title block anchored to the lower third; `plain` (`tone: "light"` in the spec) sets the same block on white; `half-image` gives the right half to a prepared authorized `image`. Keep the recommendation for the executive summary. **`section-divider`** takes one `title`, an optional `number` and `mode` (`dark` default, `light`), with the page template supplying any rules. **`agenda`** lists the deck's sections as numbered discs with labels and an optional `detail` column; `active` tints the current section's band. The composer inserts one before each section when the deck sets `agenda`.

**Page chrome** on an analytical page: `tag` prints a small grey label (*Preliminary*, *Illustrative*) right-aligned above the title; `titleLead` sets the title's first word or phrase in the accent colour; `note` and `source` share the footer line at source size with their prefixes supplied; `footer` (deck-level) prints the document title beside the page number.

**`tracker-page`** and **`tracker-label`** answer "where are we in the deck". Use no tracker for two named sections or fewer than three governed analytical slides, and keep it off the executive summary. `sequential-circles` is the default full state for three to six short peer sections; `text-agenda` suits three to six labels in a centred list; `split-contents` puts a title-only left field against a full-height right backdrop with three to eleven numbered rows and one tonal selected row. On analytical pages, `compact-label` places parent and active-child labels above the action title as one tight inline phrase, and `compact-number-strip` uses stable section numbers. Full and compact states share one label map - same IDs, order and active state - taken from the approved section map, and selection carries a non-colour cue.

## Text and synthesis

**`paragraph`** and **`bullet-list`** (`compact`, `body`) hold body copy. Items are strings or `{lead, text, icon, state, number}`; a lead is set semibold before the text on the same line. `marker` is `dot`, `number`, `icon`, `check` or `auto` (icon when any item names one, check when any carries a `state`, number when items carry leads, dot otherwise). Every marker comes from one vocabulary in `marks.mjs` - `numberMarker` (filled disc with a numeral, `reverse` for a white disc on a filled field), `iconMarker` (`outline`, `filled` or `plain`) and `stateMarker` (green tick, red cross) - so a list, a card, a table's category column and a map pin all draw the same disc. Size each bullet row from its wrapped line count, keep one bullet level where possible, use theme spacing between rows and larger tokens between groups, and let unused space fall after the final item. A wrapped bullet aligns to the start of its text.

**`icon`** draws one of the 48 named icons in `icons.mjs` (target, rocket, people, person, gear, chart-bar, chart-line, shield, clock, money, map-pin, lightbulb, checklist, handshake, building, globe, lock, search, growth, funnel, flag, star, warning, info, document, folder, mail, phone, calendar, truck, factory, leaf, bolt, cloud, database, layers, puzzle, megaphone, briefcase, wrench, refresh, link, eye, scale, check, cross, arrow-right, plus) as an editable freeform in the exported file. One icon per meaning across the deck.

**`callout`** is the page's reading aid: a cream (`color.calloutTint`) box with an optional semibold `lead` and body `text`, `tone: "caution"` adding a hairline. One per page, placed by the composer under the points column or at the foot above the takeaway.

**`insight`** holds one supported synthesis, implication or recommendation: `text`, optional substantive `heading`, optional `align`, and `variant` - `tonal` (light primary tint, default), `neutral`, `dotted` (quiet outline where a filled block would be heavy), `primary` (reserved for the deck's decisive action, carrying a chevron disc at its left). The band is left-aligned semibold body type with `space.4` side and `space.3` vertical padding. One per slide, placed after the evidence, positioned from the measured bottom of the text it concludes, and it is the last substantive content on the page. Default to one complete untitled sentence connecting the conclusion to its reason or condition.

**`evidence-note`** carries measurement bases, scope qualifiers and scenario assumptions on the same neutral surface, with `heading`, `text` and `semantic: {kind: "evidence-note", relatedTo: [exhibitId]}` naming the exhibit it decodes. **`panel`** (`open`, `muted`, `primary`, `dark`) and **`content-rail`** (`heading`, `items`, `treatment` of `open` or `muted`, optional `dividerLeft`) hold grouped supporting text beside an exhibit. An open rail takes the same frame top as its peer chart heading.

**Text containers** inherit typography, colour, line height, paragraph spacing, margins and alignment from the theme role, aligned to a deck or component guide with standardized internal margins. Use top vertical alignment for analytical text and middle alignment for compact labels and symmetric callouts. Measure headings with the resolved font before layout and pass explicit line breaks to both adapters. Padding by role: bare box `space-0` to `space-1`; compact label `space-2` horizontal and `space-1` vertical; callout `space-4` by `space-3`; panel `space-4`; action panel `space-5` by `space-4`; footer `space-0` by `space-1`.

**Inline emphasis** uses `measureTextRuns(runs, width, options)` with runs of `{text, bold, accent}` at one resolved size (`accent` sets the run in `color.accent`, which is how `titleLead` is drawn), producing one text node that HTML renders as spans and PowerPoint as runs in one box. Emphasize an exact meaningful span - a claim lead, a decisive magnitude with its unit, a qualification that changes the conclusion - and keep the whole sentence in one paragraph. Bold changes wrapping, so remeasure before compiling.

## Evidence

**`metric`** presents one to three headline values whose comparison is the evidence: `value`, `label`, optional `sublabel` and a signed `delta`, plus one explanatory line for a ratio, proxy or threshold result. `default` is the open peer row; `prominent` uses the cover-title size with body-sized labels; `tone: "dark"` fills the tile in the primary colour and `tone: "tint"` in its tint. Peers share value role, divider, label grammar and colour; one metric is emphasized only when the title explains why. A spec-level `metrics` array becomes a strip of tiles above the exhibit; a single-series chart with three categories or fewer is drawn as a column of dark tiles rather than a thin chart.

**`cards`** lays two to six pillar cards in one row, each with a `title`, `text` or `points`, an optional `icon` and `footer`. `tone` is `outline` (default when any card names an icon: centred icon, rule, title), `header` (filled header band), `numbered` (numbered disc at the head) or `plain`; the row rule holds inside the row because the cards are laid out together. **`quadrants`** draws a 2 x 2 of headed panels (`quadrants: [{title, points}]`), and the `swot` alias fills the four titles. **`framework`** draws a strategy house (`roof`, two to five `pillars` with `title` and `points`, optional `foundation`, `highlight` index for the accent pillar) or a `pyramid` (three to five `tiers`, apex first, each `label` inside the slice and `text` to its right), as one filled figure in the deck's navy and accent.

**`table`** is the typed analytical exhibit; `trend-rows`, `comparison-table` and `heatmap` are presets on the same compiler, and its cell types, treatments and header contract are set out in [charts](charts.md). Status cells: `rag` (a pill: on-track, complete, behind, at-risk, not-started), `lights` (three lamps, one lit), `progress` (a bar with its percentage), `dot` (filled or open) and `check` (tick or cross); the composer infers them from ✓/✗, status words and "40%" under a `% complete` heading. Rows may be `{style: "total"|"group", cells}` and draw as one continuous band with a bold label; `highlightColumn` (or the spec's `recommended`) tints one column; `headerShape: "chevron"` draws phase headers as chevrons; a `sectionNumber` on a category cell draws the numbered disc at the cell's left edge, in reverse on a filled cell. Column widths follow the longest content; a table over eight rows paginates with its header repeated. **`status-list`** carries compact keyed states with their labels.

**Comparison indicators** encode measured state inside cells. `completion` takes 0-100 for a true share and prints the percentage beside the mark. `traffic-light` takes `positive`, `caution`, `negative` or `missing`, each with a written trigger, a text label and a same-slide legend. `binary` takes `positive`, `negative` or `missing` with `labelDisplay` of `none` (default) or `state`, and the symbol carries the meaning. `heatmap` takes scores 1-5 with named anchors, printed in every cell, on one palette for the whole table - `theme-sequential` for a one-direction score, `red-white-green` around a genuinely neutral midpoint, `red-white` where red is an adverse condition. `ordinal-disc` (Harvey ball) takes 0-4 with defined anchors and prints the score or level. Every scale carries its legend on the same slide, and `N/A` and `Not available` stay distinct from the lowest level.

**Item indicators** number or letter repeated categories and rows: `square` for compact numbered ledgers, `circle` on a line or overlapping a field edge, `column` for a dedicated slot, `embedded-start` on the leading edge of a label field, with `inverse-keyline` as the preferred embedded treatment and `accent-fill` as the contrast alternative. Use natural integers without leading zeros; the indicator supplements the label.

**`legend`** decodes what is not printed beside every mark. Variants: `swatch` (bars, columns, pies, areas), `line` (line and combo), `marker` (scatter and bubble), `state` (actual as filled square, forecast as dashed open square, target as solid open square, scenario as dotted open circle, missing as a short line) and `quantitative-scale` (`scale: {domain, unit, palette, decimals}`, eleven shared bins with measured endpoint labels). Placement is independent: `top-right` by default for multi-series analytical charts, `bottom-center` for a coordinated group, `right` when the plot stays wide. Keep the chart's exact series order and the body-sized label role.

**`chart-callout`** attaches interpretation to an exact mark, interval, gap or threshold. `bordered` (default) is a canvas surface with a light outline and a visible triangular arrowhead; `borderless` drops the outline; `orthogonal-dot` uses a straight horizontal or vertical leader ending in a small primary dot on the keyed coordinate. Use `changeAnnotations` for a supported A-to-B change (`{start: {category, series}, end: {...}, style: "arrow", text: "+25%"}`), `referenceLines` for a decision threshold (with `placement: "outside-end"` when in-plot labels collide), `annotations` for an evidence-linked observation, and `annotationRail: {rows: [{label, items: [{category, text}]}]}` for secondary measures below the category labels. Shared constructions: endpoint arrow, interval bracket, start-to-end construction, forecast band, focal span, segment emphasis, evidence leader. Choose one primary highlight per fact; a forecast band may coexist with one leader because forecast is a data state. Change labels use the component primary with on-primary text, and change bubbles hold one value such as `+18%` or `+4.2pp`.

**`chart-group`** lays out two (`paired`), three (`triple`) or four (`four-way`) coordinated charts with equal widths, a common title band and one bottom-centred legend.

**`quote-cluster`** presents one to five sourced statements. `quotes` sets the count; `placement` says dominant or sectional; `treatment` is `contained`, `callout` or `speech-bubble`; `arrangement: "staggered"` selects the three-voice diagonal; `attributionPlacement` is `inside`, `below-center` or `below-left`; `avatar: true` adds a circular authorized portrait row below the enclosure. Use exact attributable language with speaker, role and organization, or an approved anonymized identity. Rendered-line budgets: three to seven lines for one-up, three to six for two-up, three to five for three-up, two to four for four-up, two to three for five-up.

## Media

**`image-frame`** takes an embedded `dataUri` (PNG or JPEG), nonempty `alt` and an `authorization` reference; prepare the crop at the target aspect ratio before embedding. **`icon`** uses the deck's registered library - Lucide by default - with the real vector definition, its `24 x 24` viewBox and outline grammar preserved, one icon per meaning across the deck. **`logo`** uses authorized marks at one displayed height with widths following their proportions, on a neutral backing only where contrast needs it; where a mark is unauthorized, the organization name in the label role carries the identity. **`icon-trends`** presents two to four independent trends as `columns`, `rows` or `image-columns`, each item with `id`, `title`, `text` and `media`. **`logo-collage`** shows set membership for one to twelve authorized assets, at most 80 px each, in a compact grid, `radial`, or an area-filling `collage` with normalized `cell: {x, y, width, height}`; choose `grayscale` or `color` for the whole set. Every external image, logo, icon library or source deck is registered before reuse with its source, rights reference, permitted use, required attribution, stored path and fallback.

## Relationships

**`gantt`** is the project plan: `periods` (equal columns, optionally under `tiers` of `{label, from, to}`), `rows` or `groups` of `{name, rows}` with each row `{label, from, to, text, milestones: [{at, label}], tone}`, and `today` as a fractional period index for the vertical line. Gridlines sit behind the bars, group cells fill the label column, bar text sits inside the bar and milestone diamonds carry their labels beside them. **`cycle`** places three to six labelled steps around a ring of chevrons with an optional `center` statement; **`steps`** is a milestone staircase of three to six `{label, text}`; **`people`** is a row of two to five profile cards (`name`, `role`, up to three `points`, optional `image`); **`logos`** is a wall of two to twelve authorized marks with captions.

**`process`** puts ordered steps on one equal-interval rail, with stage objects of `label` and optional `period`, `maturity` and short `detail`; an active index changes the marker state, not the spacing. **`chevron-process`** uses compact overlapping bands with spaced bullet rows, and `detailStyle: "circled-number"` when sequence matters. **`initiative-rollout`** and **`highlight-strip`** carry keyed items along a shared band.

**`roadmap`** shows ordered stage bands with a declared period or maturity basis: `process` for equal ordinal spacing, `wave-columns` for developed activity and deliverable lists that align across stages, and `phase-workstreams` for an ordinal programme with nested workstreams. That variant takes `phases[]` (`id`, `label`, optional `period`, `workstreams[]`, `products[]`), workstreams with keyed `activities[]` and an optional exact-prefix `lead`, products with `workstreamId`, optional `owner` and recursive `children[]`, up to two keyed `owners[]`, `leadershipBands[]` with contiguous `phaseIds`, `recurringProducts[]`, `qualifiers[]` with `appliesTo`, and optional `workstreamWeights` for label space. Equal phase widths encode order; approximate periods stay labels.

**`timeline`** places dated events on a declared time scale. `time-grid` takes `axis: {kind: "periods", precision, periods}`, keyed lanes and `eventKinds` with legend labels and `triangle` or `open-circle` symbols. `dated-lanes` takes `axis: {kind: "date", scale: "elapsed-days", precision: "day", domain}` with events keyed to an exact lane and date, positioned by elapsed days; a continuous activity declares `extent` and an `extentQualification`. `phase-hierarchy` uses equal-width parent phases with source-authored `duration` strings and one `expandedPhaseId` whose keyed children appear below. `labelTreatment: "prominent"` binds primary schedule evidence to the heading role, and `spacing: "tight"` uses `space.1` internal clearance without changing type size.

**`journey`** gives one actor, ordered stages and stage-aligned touchpoints. **`tree`** takes one root, stable child IDs and explicit parent-child links; the `decision-conclusions` variant takes `root`, two `branches` with one to three identified `conclusions`, and one overall `conclusion` across three visible node layers, in at least 900 x 440. **`organization`** shows one accountable root role, reporting layers and stable role IDs, where size carries no rank. **`matrix`** takes `xAxis` and `yAxis`, each with `label`, `minLabel` and `maxLabel`, with coordinates from 0 to 1. **`funnel`** takes ordered stages, one denominator and proportional widths with values and conversion rates; `variant: "qualitative"` with `basis: "qualitative-selection"` communicates successive selection states schematically, with `inputSources`, `inputDescription` and a distinct `outcome`, and no counts implied.

**`map`** renders editable native country polygons from Natural Earth 1:110m. Set `geography` to a preset (`world`, `usa`, `usa-contiguous`, `europe`, `emea`, `apac`, `mena`, `gcc`, `latin-america`, single countries, or `country:ISO`) or to a sourced object `{id, title, source: {url, license, sha256}, geojson, bounds?}`. `highlightCountries` marks the geographies carrying the claim in component primary; `markers` accept a country anchor or crop-relative `{x, y}` from the displayed crop's top-left, or `longitude`/`latitude` for sourced GeoJSON; a marker with a `number` draws the numbered disc pin that a list or table beside the map can key to. `choropleth: {values, scale, labels}` fills features from one explicit domain on the shared eleven-bin heat scale. Regional acronym membership varies by institution, so state the declared country set beside any analytical claim.

**`connector`** draws a real directional relationship between two named objects: `disc-chevron` (preferred for a row-level implication), `chevron` (light, between two open reasoning lists), `line`, and `labelled-line`. Bind the disc to `icon-md`, align it to the centres of the fields it joins, and reserve whitespace for it. Use standalone implication chevrons at one or two deliberate emphasis points per deck; process chevrons that encode real stages are a separate use.

**`insight-tree-table`** aligns a root finding, one to four named branches, two to seven leaves, and one insight plus one to four implications per leaf, under three substantive headers, with `rowTreatment` of `tonal` or `open`. **`relationship-network`** (`hub-ring`, `directed-spokes`) arranges one `centerId` and three to six perimeter `nodes` in a named `ringOrder`, with `edges` naming exact `from`, `to`, a semantic `relation` and `direction` of `forward`, `bidirectional` or `none`; supported edges are centre spokes and adjacent perimeter pairs.

## Check

Every component has an audience job; repeated components resolve to one definition and variant; labels and colours carry real meaning; each one stays subordinate to the title and the dominant exhibit; and every object stays editable in the exported file.
