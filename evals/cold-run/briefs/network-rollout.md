# Brief: sequencing a rollout across twelve markets

An operations director has twelve markets and eighteen months, and cannot start
everywhere. Which markets go first, and what would change the order.

- **Audience.** An executive committee, approving a sequence and a budget.
- **Decision.** The first wave, the gate that opens the second, and the owner of
  each.
- **Ranked criteria.** Readiness of the local data; cost to serve; regulatory
  lead time; the size of the prize.
- **Constraints.** Readiness is a qualitative assessment on a four-point scale,
  not a number. Two markets have no data at all and must stay visible as open
  rather than being scored as zero.
- **Length.** About 20 pages.

Why this brief is in the suite: a twelve-row scorecard on a four-point scale is
the page the skill has always been able to draw and almost never does. A good
run paginates the long table, draws the rating column as harvey balls, and keeps
the two unknown markets legible as unknown.
