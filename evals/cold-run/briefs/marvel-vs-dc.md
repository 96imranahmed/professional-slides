# Brief: Marvel versus DC

A reader wants to know which of Marvel and DC is "better", and has not said on
what. Settle what the question can honestly mean, answer it, and say where the
answer changes.

- **Audience.** One general reader, deciding what to watch and read next.
- **Decision.** Which universe to start with, and where to start inside it.
- **Constraints.** Commercial claims must be measured; artistic claims must be
  marked as judgement. Box-office gross is not profit.
- **Length.** About 50 pages, including cover, dividers and back matter.

Why this brief is in the suite: it is the run that exposed the craft gap. It
passed every gate the skill had in September 2026 — 44% charts, 22% tables,
0.888 entropy, nine architectures — and produced ten three-row tables with no
treatment, eighteen charts with no annotation and not one highlighted phrase.
Nothing about the brief is at fault. It is a perfectly ordinary comparison, and
that is the point: this is what the skill does when nobody is watching.
