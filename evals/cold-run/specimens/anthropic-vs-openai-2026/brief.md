# Anthropic versus OpenAI

Audience: investors and strategy leaders. Decision stage: comparative diagnosis, not a trade recommendation.

Question: Who is better positioned in the short run and long run, and which company has the stronger capital structure?

Answer: OpenAI leads direct consumer reach and disclosed funding access; Anthropic leads selected paid enterprise signals and the latest measured premium-model benchmark. Neither has a provably superior overall capital structure or a defensible long-run win on public evidence.

Horizon: short run means 12–24 months; long run means three to seven years. Rank criteria separately: direct reach, enterprise retained work, task quality, task economics, capital access, and balance-sheet quality. Reversal condition: observed paid retention and gross margin change the long-run call; matched usage and task outcomes change segment leadership.

Evidence status: company disclosures, SEC counterparty filings, independent measurement, investor survey estimates, press reporting, and labeled analyst inference remain distinct.
