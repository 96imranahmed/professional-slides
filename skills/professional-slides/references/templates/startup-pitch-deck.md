# Startup pitch deck

## Mandate

Use when founders must convince investors that a problem, product, market, team and financing plan support the next stage of company building. Choose the delivery mode first - live pitch, investor pre-read or short teaser - and take the matching density profile: `live-pitch` when the words are spoken, `pre-read` when the deck is read unattended.

## Decision question

> Why should this company be funded now, and what milestones will the round buy?

## Page table

| Page | Job | Exhibit | Evidence it owes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cover | Name the company and the round context | Cover | Stage, evidence cutoff, the decision being asked for |
| Problem and urgency | Show the user problem and why existing options fail | Journey, product flow or before-and-after | The user, the failure of current options, why now |
| Product | Show how the product changes the experience | Product flow, screenshots or demonstration frames | What the product does, for whom, at what step |
| Demand proof | Show that people use and keep using it | Cohort retention, growth or conversion chart | Dated, labelled usage, growth, retention, revenue or qualified pipeline |
| Market and competition | Define the reachable market and why the company wins | Sized market exhibit plus a comparison against real alternatives | Reachable market rather than headline TAM, buyer-relevant criteria |
| Business model and go-to-market | Show how use becomes revenue and contribution | Unit economics or funnel | Pricing, acquisition, sales motion, supply, channels, network dynamics |
| Scale and risk | Show what improves with scale and what breaks first | Driver or sensitivity exhibit | Risks tied to milestones rather than a generic risk matrix |
| Team | Show why these people | Team exhibit with evidence of ability | The role each person plays in the next stage, and the relevant track record |
| Round and milestones | State the raise and what it buys | Milestone table against use of funds | Capital linked to measurable product, growth, operating or market milestones |

Merge jobs when the story is short. A live pitch may omit a visible tracker and a standalone summary when the opening sequence already makes the case.

## Evidence

Separate fact, company claim, estimate and inference, and label company-reported or unaudited measures as such. Cite public or supplied evidence. Calibrate claims to the stage, so an early signal reads as an early signal. Downloads, press and a few logos measure attention rather than retention.

## Failure checks

Reject when: the problem or product is vague; traction is undated or unsourced; the market is only a headline TAM; the business model is missing; risks are generic; team credibility rests on logos; the round lacks milestones; a live pitch uses dense pre-read layouts; visual repetition stands in for evidence.

## Acceptance check

Problem, product, demand proof, market, business model, scale logic, risks, team, round and milestones are all clear, and the exported deck passes the page gates and the review.
