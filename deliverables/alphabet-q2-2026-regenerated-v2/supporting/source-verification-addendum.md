# Source verification after production story review 02

Source S2 remains the originally admitted Alphabet 30 June 2026 10-Q, MD&A page 48: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000071/goog-20260630.htm

The filing explicitly attributes the group TAC-rate decrease to the following cause:

> primarily due to a revenue mix shift from Google Network properties to Google Search & other properties.

It separately reports that the Search TAC rate was substantially consistent. Thus the review's claim that the filing does not identify the group-rate driver is contradicted by the primary source. Production retains the mix fact with explicit issuer attribution rather than presenting it as an analyst causal inference.

Other review repairs remain valid: the capex/depreciation gap alone does not measure incremental capacity; financing proceeds are not traced to specific growth expenditure; the cash/marketable-debt definition deliberately excludes all equity and does not establish that every excluded holding is immediately unavailable. Current non-SpaceX equity is 87.063 minus 80.000, or 7.063 billion; its availability is unassessed. These bounded clarifications change no scenario calculation.
